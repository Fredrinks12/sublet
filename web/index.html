Let yours

          
Already paying for a box that sits idle? This is all it takes.

        

        
    
          
  1. 
            
    Publish your terms
    
            
    Name your price, the Unix user tenants log in as, and how long a tick lasts. The terms live in the contract, where anyone can read them.
    
            
    sublet list helsinki-8c terms.json
    
          
    2. 
          
  2. 
            
    Trust one key
    
            
    The contract gets its own key for your machine, derived by the MPC network. No person ever holds it.
    
            
    echo 'ssh-ed25519 AAAA…' | sudo tee /etc/ssh/sublet_ca.pub
echo 'TrustedUserCAKeys /etc/ssh/sublet_ca.pub' \
  | sudo tee /etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/sublet.conf
sudo systemctl reload ssh
    
          
    3. 
          
  3. 
            
    Get paid directly
    
            
    Rent on Base goes straight to your own address. Rent in NEAR is forwarded the moment a key exists. The contract never keeps your money.

What happens when you pay

          
Paying in USDC on Base. No bridge, no solver and no oracle. Each row is one step.

        

        

          

            BaseNEAR contractMPC networkYour server
          

          
1 Tenant sends USDC to the owner's address.

          
2 Anyone submits the claim. The payer's signature ties it to one SSH key, so a watcher cannot steal it.

          
3 Nodes read the transfer from Base and sign what they found.

          
4 Contract rebuilds the hash from the claim. Token, owner and amount must all match.

          
5 Contract writes the certificate from the owner's terms: who, for how long, and which command.

          
6 Nodes sign those exact bytes. No single node ever holds the key.

          
7 Stock OpenSSH checks the signature, offline, and lets the tenant in.

          
8 Time's up. The session ends and the key stops working.
